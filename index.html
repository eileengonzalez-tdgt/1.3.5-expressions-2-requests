01 · Encounter: How Would You Ask?

Look at each situation. Describe what is happening and suggest what the person could say. Then compare a direct request with a more polite version.

🕒 Everyday life · Asking for the time

Your phone battery is dead and you need to catch a bus. Someone is standing nearby.

Discuss: What would you say? How could you make your question sound friendly?

🎧 Customer support · Requesting information

A customer calls because an order has not arrived. The agent needs the order number before checking.

Discuss: How should the agent ask for the information politely?

Talk with a partner

  1. What makes the two requests polite?
  2. Does adding please always make a sentence natural?
  3. How does the audience change the language you choose?
Notice: Polite requests are clear, considerate, and appropriate to the situation.

02 · Clarify: Requests That Sound Natural

Explore 12 practical expressions. Select a card to reveal its example and context. Practice the pronunciation and use each phrase in a new situation.

Remember: This is an introduction to request expressions. You will study modal grammar in more detail on Day 4.

Three useful patterns

Could / Can + subject + base verb?

“Could you help me with this?”

Would you mind + verb-ing?

“Would you mind waiting a moment?”

Could you tell me + indirect question?

“Could you tell me where the bank is?”

Not: “where is the bank” after tell me.

Would it be possible to + base verb?

“Would it be possible to reschedule?”

Respond to the request

Accept

“Of course!” · “Sure, no problem.”

Decline politely

“I'm sorry, but I can't right now.”

Useful lesson vocabulary: bring a dish → “Could you bring a dish?” · sign up on → “Could you sign up on the website?” · let us know → “Could you let us know by Friday?”

Speaking check: Change “Give me your email address” into a polite professional request, then respond naturally.

03 · Remember: Word Order Challenge

Select the scrambled word tiles in the correct order. Build ten polite requests, check each answer, and practice saying it aloud with a partner.

04 · Internalize: Practice in Context

Work with a partner. Switch roles after each conversation. Make at least two requests and respond appropriately.

🍽️ At a restaurant

Role A: Customer who needs a menu recommendation and the bill.

Role B: Server.

Reveal guiding phrases

“Could you recommend something?” · “Could we have the bill, please?”

🗺️ Asking for directions

Role A: Visitor looking for the train station.

Role B: Local resident.

Reveal guiding phrases

“Do you know where the station is?” · “Could you give me directions?”

📚 Borrowing an item

Role A: Student who forgot a pen.

Role B: Classmate.

Reveal guiding phrases

“Could I borrow your pen?” · “Would you mind lending me one?”

🎧 Workplace extension

Role A: Support agent asking for a screenshot.

Role B: Customer.

Reveal guiding phrases

“Could you send us a screenshot, please?” · “Would you mind describing the error?”

Partner feedback

Was the request clear? Was the tone appropriate? Did the other person respond naturally?

05 · Fluent Use: Spin the Wheel

Spin for a surprise situation. With a partner, act out a 30–45 second conversation: open politely, make a request, respond naturally, and ask a follow-up. Change roles and spin again. All ten situations will appear before any repeat.
